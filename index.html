Questions

Good to know

Do you have dairy-free milk?

Yes. We have oat, almond and coconut milk at no extra charge, because we think that charge is silly.

Can I buy beans ground?

Absolutely. Tell us how you brew (drip, French press, espresso or pour over) and we'll grind it to match.

Do you cater events?

We do. Our espresso cart can come to offices, weddings and events anywhere along the Wasatch Front.

Are you hiring?

Often! Stop by either shop with a résumé, or email us below.
